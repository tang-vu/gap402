# Motion storyboard

The visual object is one physical dossier: warm rough paper with a thin cut aperture, an amber translucent annotation leaf, three numbered slips and a dark receipt strip. The paper is approximately 1 mm thick in the illustration, with a broad upper-left key light, restrained ambient fill and contact shadows. Source identity and all money stay as selectable DOM text. Motion is illustrative and makes no claim of live execution.

## Timeline map

| Scene | Time / progress | Physical action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | 0–0.8 s | Paper rises from its offset stack; camera tilt and contact shadow settle. |
| Hero | 0.8–1.8 s | The aperture leaf opens with registration marks and exposes the unresolved gap. |
| Hero | 1.8–3.3 s | Three persistent slips travel on different vectors, rotations and depths. |
| Hero | 3.3–4.4 s | Curved connectors trace to the inspection boundary without granting acceptance. |
| Hero | 4.4–5 s | The receipt strip settles as an illustrative destination; controls and scroll cue remain available. |
| Story | 0–20% Request | The same dossier approaches an inspection plane; aperture and declared criteria align. |
| Story | 20–48% Inspect | Slips advance; the stale third slip turns toward a rejected lane. The duplicate is described as prevented, so it is not invented as a fourth source. |
| Story | 48–76% Allocate | Connectors draw to a ribbon with supplier, verifier and refund destinations. Exact values are deferred to the returned lab result. |
| Story | 76–100% Record | The receipt strip and source identity come forward. Reverse scroll reverses the same timeline. |
| Lab | 0–10 s | A selected returned slip moves into inspection, recorded evaluation appears, the actual payout and recipient are exposed, then its receipt status is shown. Direct chapter and seek controls address the same timeline. Blocked results stop at the unmet requirement. |

At mobile widths, the story stage remains sticky at under half the viewport while chapters scroll behind it. Direct controls seek those same positions by touch. Reduced motion presents the final static composition and readable data.

## Reference inspection

The four requested sites returned HTTP 200 in Chromium. [Bearplus](https://bear.plus/) uses a large persistent mascot beside a project register; [Silana](https://www.silana.com/) opens with a broad dotted cyan form against black; [HeronAI](https://heronaiapp.com/) keeps an architectural drawing framed as an inspection surface; [United Carriers](https://unitedcarriers.com/) presented a very dark scroll field during this capture. Four viewport frames and a scroll recording were saved for each site under `docs/images/reference-motion`. These captures establish the visible sequence in this browser session, but do not establish hidden camera parameters or behavior on every device. The implementation uses an original paper dossier composition.
